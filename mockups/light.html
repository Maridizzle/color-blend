The Lit Archive

Everything that is already there stays: the reliquary, the vellum, the gold and wine, the blackletter. What this adds is light. Dust drifting in candlelight, gilding that sweeps across what you have earned, a halo on the next thing to do, sparks when a tile lands, and a bloom when the picture settles.

Nothing here is built yet. Flip between Proposed and Current to compare, and click a tile on the board to see a landing. The paintings are stand‑in gradients; none of your artwork is shown.

What changes, screen by screen

Soft, slow, and always below the reading. Nothing flashes.

  • ✦
    Candle dustA handful of motes drift up through every screen, gold and moon‑blue, each twinkling on its own clock. Drawn on a canvas, so it costs nothing on a phone.
  • ✦
    The next archive breathesA halo swells and fades on the station you would play next, so the road tells you where you are without a label.
  • ✦
    A finished archive is gildedA thin thread of gold light travels the rim of a closed reliquary, once around every six seconds.
  • ✦
    A bead on the roadOne point of light walks the road line top to bottom and fades, every eight seconds: the road runs on.
  • ✦
    Held pieces twinkleIn each station's arch, the pieces you have earned brighten one by one, out of step, like embers.
  • ✦
    Sheen on what you holdA soft diagonal light crosses each held piece every eleven seconds, staggered across the plate, as if a candle passed behind you.
  • ✦
    Gilding a whole pictureOnce the archive is whole, a slow gold sweep crosses the entire plate every nine seconds. Earned once, and it keeps being earned.
  • ✦
    Sealed sigils breatheThe ✦ on each unopened piece warms to gold and back, out of step, so a half‑done plate feels alive rather than blank.
  • ✦
    Chapter endings glowThe "End of Chapter" cite carries a slow gold glow. The cliffhanger should look like the most important line on the page, because it is.
  • ✦
    Tiles catch the lightA faint specular gradient sits on every tile, so they read as lacquered stone rather than flat colour. The colours themselves are untouched, so the sort is exactly as fair.
  • ✦
    The carried tile glowsThe tile in your hand gains a warm rim light beneath its shadow, and the cell it will land in presses down, as now.
  • ✦
    Sparks on landingWhen a tile drops into place, a small burst of gold and moon sparks scatters and dies in under a second. Click a tile to see it. Correct placements only, so the sparks mean something.
  • ✦
    Fact tiles shimmerA tile that hides a fact carries a faint gold ring that breathes, so a player can learn to look for them.
  • ✦
    A bloom behind the pictureAs the artwork settles, a soft warm light swells behind it and breathes slowly for as long as you look.
  • ✦
    Sparkles around the frameA few points of gold and moon light appear and fade around the picture, never on it, so nothing competes with the art itself.
  • ✦
    The Read on button shimmersA slow light sweep crosses the primary button every few seconds, a quiet invitation rather than a nag.
  • ✦
    Screens cross‑fadeRoad to gallery to folio to board no longer cut; each fades and settles into the next over half a second.

In every case

Transform and opacity only, so it stays smooth on an old phone. Motes are one canvas at about forty particles. Every effect switches off under the reduced‑motion setting, in the system and in the game's own Settings.

The board's colours are never altered by any of this, so nothing about the puzzle changes; only the light around it does.
